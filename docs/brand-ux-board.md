# Shattab Assistant UX Board

The assistant follows Shattab's existing Modern Heritage design system rather
than introducing a separate chat visual language.

- Arabic and RTL are the default; English remains available through the app's
  generated localization files.
- The entry point is the existing shell FAB with an accessible tooltip.
- The empty state offers short Egyptian-Arabic starter prompts for common
  finishing questions.
- Replies are compact cards with a clear follow-up question, quick actions,
  and a persistent privacy note.
- Homeowner recommendations are real discovery cards. Names, ratings,
  verification badges, and navigation destinations are read from the existing
  marketplace listing model.
- The professional portal receives industry and app guidance but does not show
  a fabricated list of other professionals.
- Loading, retry, empty, and reduced-motion behavior use existing Batsh atoms
  and motion tokens.

## Homeowner home browsing

The accepted homeowner reference keeps its scoped Tajawal, navy and orange
identity. Preserve the hero, services, project and assistant geometry. Lower
discovery groups have 24dp separation with headings close to their content.
Professional rails use 252dp tiles, 4:3 rounded media and clean readable bodies;
completed work uses a 3:2 editorial image. Avoid repeating faint full-card frames.
Keep truthful sponsored treatment and independent profile, save and quote actions.
Known absent professional cover photos omit the media rectangle and use a compact
identity body with a visible save action; nonempty URLs retain honest fallbacks.

Community is one subtle cream destination with at most two real posts, its
heading, recovery state and write action. Photo posts use 4:3 media; text-only
posts omit media entirely. A single post fills the available section width. Stack
two tiles at narrow widths or large text scale.
Authors and names use at least 16px, supporting text at least 14px with 1.3 line
height, and controls keep 48dp hit areas. Captions may preview three lines only
when their full text remains available to accessibility and the detail route.
