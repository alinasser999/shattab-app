## 1. What we collect

### Everyone

| Data | Why | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Phone number | It is your account identifier and how the other party contacts you | You, at sign-up |
| Name | Shown on your profile, briefs and quotes | You |
| Profile photo | Shown on your profile | You, optional |
| Account role (homeowner or contractor) | Determines which parts of the app you see | Chosen at sign-up |
| Email address and name | Only if you use Google or Apple sign-in | Google / Apple |

If you sign in with Apple and choose **Hide My Email**, we receive only Apple's
relay address and never see your real one.

### Homeowners

Job requests you post: work description, apartment type, governorate, city,
district, and any photos you attach. Quotes you receive, which contractor you
hired, and reviews you write.

### Contractors

Business name, bio, headline, specialties, service areas, years of experience.
Portfolio projects, including titles, descriptions, locations, years and photos.
Quotes you send. Reviews about you.

If you apply for the **verified** badge, the identity or trade documents you
upload. If you pay for a subscription, the transfer reference and any payment
screenshot you upload.

### Everyone, if you use the feed

Posts, captions and photos you publish; comments you write; posts you like or
save; and any reports you file or accounts you block.

### Collected automatically

Crash and error diagnostics. We have configured our crash reporting to **not**
attach personal information: no user identifiers, no request bodies, no
screenshots. We receive the technical details of the failure only.

We do **not** collect location data, contacts, advertising identifiers, or
browsing activity outside the app. We do not use tracking for advertising, and
we do not sell personal data.

## 2. Why we use it

- To run the marketplace: showing your brief to matching contractors, delivering
  quotes, letting you compare and hire.
- To let the two sides contact each other. Contractors publish their phone
  number as a business contact. **Homeowners' phone numbers are not shown in the
  public feed.**
- To display reviews and the verified badge, so trust is legible.
- To process subscription payments.
- To keep the platform safe: reviewing reports, acting on abuse, and enforcing
  the rules in our Terms.
- To fix crashes.

We rely on performing our contract with you for the marketplace functions, on
your consent for optional uploads such as photos, and on our legitimate interest
in a safe and functioning platform for moderation and diagnostics.

## 3. Who can see what

**Public within the app:** your name, profile photo, role, and — for contractors
— business details, portfolio, ratings and reviews, and business phone number.
Posts you publish are visible to other users.

**Limited:** your brief is visible to contractors matching its category and area,
and to any contractor you send it to directly. Your quote is visible to the
homeowner who received it.

**Private:** verification documents and payment proofs are stored in private
buckets and are readable only by you and by us for review. Blocks are private —
the person you block is never told.

We share data with service providers only:

Your data is stored in the EU. We may disclose data where legally required.

Calling or messaging someone through the app opens your own phone or WhatsApp.
Those conversations happen outside Shattab and are governed by that app's own
policy — we neither see nor store them. **Shattab has no in-app chat.**

## 4. How long we keep it

Account data is kept while your account exists. Delete your account and it is
removed as described below. Reports may be retained after the reported content
is gone, because a moderation record that vanishes with the content is useless.
We keep records needed for tax or legal obligations for as long as the law
requires.

## 5. Your rights and controls

**Delete your account, in the app:** Profile → Settings → Delete account. This
is immediate and permanent. It erases your profile, briefs, quotes, posts,
comments, likes, saves, photos, uploaded documents, and reviews written about
you. It cannot be undone and we cannot restore it.

Reviews you wrote about contractors are attached to your job requests and are
deleted along with them; the affected contractors' rating averages are
recalculated automatically.

You can also edit your profile at any time, block any user, and report content.

Depending on where you live you may have rights to access, correct, export or
restrict processing of your data. Write to {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}} and we will respond
within 30 days.

## 6. Children

Shattab is not intended for anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect
data from children. If you believe a child has an account, contact
{{SUPPORT_EMAIL}} and we will remove it.

## 7. Security

Data is encrypted in transit. Access is enforced at the database level with
row-level security, so one account cannot read another's private data. Sensitive
uploads sit in private storage. No system is perfectly secure, but we treat
account-boundary bugs as our highest-severity class of problem.
